| XTPU-0055 | open | high | m | XTPU-0049, XTPU-0054 | The applet presents all nine profiles as runnable, but only `low-light-enhancement` declares `requirements.model`, and `src/omnitensor/plugins/orchestration.py` refuses to build a pipeline runner for a profile with no model (`profile-has-no-model`). | XTPU-0057 | open | medium | s | XTPU-0058 | `manifestVersion` drift: the applet pins `const 1` in `workload-manifest.schema.json` and `MANIFEST_VERSION` in `lib/workload-manifest.js`, while `omnitensor/schemas/workload-manifest.schema.json` accepts `enum [1, 2]` and `plugins/manifest_compatibility.py` actively rewrites manifests to version 2. | XTPU-0059 | open | medium | xs | XTPU-0057 | `requirements.model.sha256` (digest pinning the exact artifact a profile may run) exists in the service schema but is rejected by the applet: `lib/workload-manifest.js` `MODEL_PROPERTIES` is an exact-count set of six names and `runtime-snapshot`-style `additionalProperties: false` applies in the schema mirror. | XTPU-0060 | open | medium | s | XTPU-0056 | The applet cannot tell an absent service from an incompatible one. `lib/manager.js` `controlFailureText` substring-matches `ServiceUnknown`/`NameHasNoOwner` and `TimedOut`/`Timeout`, and collapses everything else — `UnknownMethod` from an older service, a wrong reply signature, a contract violation, a command-id mismatch, every guard refusal — into one sentence. There is no version handshake, no introspection, and no `NameOwnerChanged` watch, so the applet also never notices the service starting or stopping. |
| XTPU-0061 | open | medium | s | — | Every applet restart wastes one control round trip. `lib/manager.js` initialises `_runtimeRevision` to 0 and only updates it from an acknowledgement, but the runtime snapshot carries no `revision` field on either side, so the first command after startup always sends `expectedRevision: 0`, is rejected by `omnitensor/src/omnitensor/control.py` with "Runtime policy revision changed; refresh and retry", and only then learns the real revision. Publish the revision in the snapshot or resynchronise before the first command. |
| XTPU-0062 | open | low | xs | — | Nothing verifies that the two halves agree on the snapshot path. Add a gate that fails when the defaults drift. |
| XTPU-0063 | open | low | xs | — | Smaller snapshot and manifest schema drift, all applet-side omissions: `profiles.maxProperties: 128` is absent from `runtime-snapshot.schema.json` and from `lib/runtime-snapshot-schema-validator.js`, and the version 1/2 `oneOf` discriminator is absent from `workload-manifest.schema.json`. | XTPU-0064 | open | low | s | XTPU-0055 | Snapshot content addressed to unknown ids disappears without a trace: `lib/domain.js` drops alerts whose `profileId` is not in the local `WorkloadCatalog` and copies only profiles that match a catalog definition, while the service validates `profileId` merely as a well-formed plugin id and will publish alerts for plugins the applet does not ship.
