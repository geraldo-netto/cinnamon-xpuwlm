| XTPU-0070 | open | medium | s | XTPU-0069 | The applet ships a `visual-library/manifest.json` that disagrees with the service's copy: the applet declares `requirements.model: null` and `accelerator: tpu`, while `omnitensor/workloads/visual-library/manifest.json` declares an ncnn model (`visual-library-classifier` 1.1.0, with `sha256`) and `accelerator: gpu`.
