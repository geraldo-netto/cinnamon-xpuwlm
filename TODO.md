| XTPU-0052 | open | high | l | XTPU-0053 | The applet cannot report a job outcome because it consumes none of the runtime's result surface. | XTPU-0053 | open | high | m | XTPU-0052 | The applet calls only `ApplyCommand` (`lib/cinnamon-runtime.js` `CONTROL_METHOD`). `SubmitJob`, `CancelJob`, `DescribePlugins`, and `GetJobResult` are exported by the service and never called, and the applet ships no mirror of `runtime-job-submit`, `runtime-job-cancel`, `runtime-job-acknowledgement`, `runtime-job-result-request`, `runtime-job-result`, or `plugin-inventory`. Nothing in the applet can start, cancel, or observe work; it is a policy editor over a service that can run jobs. |
| XTPU-0054 | open | high | m | XTPU-0055 | Nothing can grant the permissions the service's policy gate requires. `src/omnitensor/plugins/policy.py` refuses with `consent-missing` when a required permission has no active grant, but at audit time `plugins/grants.py` `GrantLedger` was never constructed by the service and had no default path, CLI entry point, environment variable, or D-Bus method, and `runtime-command.schema.json` `operation` is closed over `set-profile-enabled, set-profile-weight, set-paused`. The applet has no grant UI or storage. Needs a grant interface agreed with OmniTensor before any applet work; currently latent only because no bundled manifest declares `permissions`. |
| XTPU-0055 | open | high | m | XTPU-0049, XTPU-0054 | The applet presents all nine profiles as runnable, but only `low-light-enhancement` declares `requirements.model`, and `src/omnitensor/plugins/orchestration.py` refuses to build a pipeline runner for a profile with no model (`profile-has-no-model`). Eight of nine bundled workloads can never run, and the ninth is disabled by default pending XTPU-0049. The popup must state which profiles the runtime cannot execute instead of offering identical enable/weight controls for all of them. |
| XTPU-0062 | open | low | xs | — | Nothing verifies that the two halves agree on the snapshot path. Add a gate that fails when the defaults drift. |
| XTPU-0065 | open | high | m | XTPU-0052 | Cross-repository, owned by OmniTensor and being addressed there: at audit time `SubmitJob` dispatched through `_job_dispatcher` without touching `RunnerSet.runners`, `_default_dispatcher` returned `UnavailableJobDispatcher` unless an artifact root was configured, `build_plugin_runners` was called without `deliver=` so delivery was a no-op, and `_describe_plugins` never passed `granted_permissions`, so every inventory permission reported `granted: false`. Re-verify against the service before starting the applet-side job work in XTPU-0052 and XTPU-0053; `GetJobResult` and uid-scoped job ownership have since landed. |
| XTPU-0066 | open | low | s | — | No version handshake with the runtime service. | XTPU-0067 | open | low | xs | — | `lib/workload-manifest.js` treats an own property whose value is `undefined` as present, so `requirements.acceleratorPreference: undefined` and `requirements.model.sha256: undefined` are rejected while the authoritative schema treats both as absent.
