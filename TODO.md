| XTPU-0060 | open | medium | s | — | The applet cannot tell an absent service from an incompatible one. `lib/manager.js` `controlFailureText` substring-matches `ServiceUnknown`/`NameHasNoOwner` and `TimedOut`/`Timeout`, and collapses everything else — `UnknownMethod` from an older service, a wrong reply signature, a contract violation, a command-id mismatch, every guard refusal — into one sentence. | XTPU-0061 | open | medium | s | — | Every applet restart wastes one control round trip. `lib/manager.js` initialises `_runtimeRevision` to 0 and only updates it from an acknowledgement, but the runtime snapshot carries no `revision` field on either side, so the first command after startup always sends `expectedRevision: 0`, is rejected by `omnitensor/src/omnitensor/control.py` with "Runtime policy revision changed; refresh and retry", and only then learns the real revision.
