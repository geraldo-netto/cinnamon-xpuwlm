| XTPU-0053 | blocked | high | m | XTPU-0052, XTPU-0069 | Blocked on a decision, with the reason now identified rather than assumed. Producing one for `visual-library` means decoding a picture, resizing it to the model's input, and applying that model's normalisation — none of which the manifest states: `omnitensor` OMNI-0192 records that no manifest carries a tensor contract and that inferring one from the ncnn `.param` would refuse jobs that succeed today. So a submission surface waits on OMNI-0192 (a stated tensor contract) and OMNI-0196 (who converts a PNG into a raw buffer). `DescribeContract` is now called (`lib/runtime-contract.js`); `DescribePlugins` is deliberately not, because after `omnitensor` OMNI-0204 its per-profile artifact readiness says exactly what `profiles[].reason` already says, and plumbing with no consumer is how the runner layer sat unreachable for a release. |
